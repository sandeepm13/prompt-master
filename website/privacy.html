Privacy Policy for dotix

Last updated: November 20, 2026

dotix ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when you use the dotix desktop application and website.

1. Information Processing & Data Minimization

When you request a prompt rewrite, the text in your focused text field is processed temporarily to generate an improved prompt. The application uses the Windows clipboard for a brief moment solely to read and replace the prompt text, immediately restoring your previous clipboard content. No prompts, keystrokes, or text snippets are logged or stored on any intermediary server.

2. Third-Party AI Providers

dotix connects directly from your device to the AI service provider you configure (such as Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, or a local Ollama instance). Transmission of prompt text occurs directly between your computer and the chosen provider's official API, subject to their respective privacy terms.

3. Local Storage of Credentials

Your API keys, custom prompt styles, and settings are stored locally on your device in your user app data directory (%APPDATA%\MasterPrompt\config.toml). They are never transmitted to us or any third party.

4. Telemetry & Analytics

The dotix desktop application contains no telemetry, analytics tracking, or user profiling tools. We do not collect personal identifiers, email addresses, or usage logs.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository: github.com/sandeepm13/prompt-master.
